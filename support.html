Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Where are my drafts stored?

Entirely on your own device. The Echo Sink has no account system and no cloud sync — your drafts never leave your phone.

What happens when a cooling timer finishes?

You'll get a gentle notification. Open the Reservoir tab and the draft will be unblurred and ready to copy wherever you'd like to send it.

Can I delete a draft before it unlocks?

Yes — swipe or tap the trash icon on any card in the Cooling Reservoir at any time, whether it's still cooling or already unlocked.

Can I change the cooling period after I've sunk a draft?

Not currently — the timer is set when you sink the draft. You can choose 15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, or 24 hours before sinking.

Does The Echo Sink collect any of my data?

No. There's no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is transmitted anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I found a bug, or have a suggestion

We'd love to hear from you. Email stephaniestreeting@gmail.com with as much detail as you can — including your device model and iOS version if it's a bug — and we'll get back to you.

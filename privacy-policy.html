The Echo Sink

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026
The short version: The Echo Sink does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you write stays only on your own device.

What data we collect

None. The Echo Sink has no backend, no servers, and no cloud storage. There is nothing for us to collect, because nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

Where your drafts are stored

Every draft you write is saved locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks. It is never uploaded, synced, backed up to a server we control, or shared with anyone — including us. If you delete the app, your drafts are deleted along with it.

Notifications

When a draft finishes cooling, The Echo Sink schedules a local notification directly through iOS. This happens entirely on your device and does not involve any push notification service, server, or third party.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The Echo Sink contains no analytics software development kits (SDKs), no advertising networks, and no tracking of any kind. We do not know how you use the app, how often you open it, or what you write in it.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any data to share.

Children's privacy

Because The Echo Sink does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 or any other age.

Your control over your data

Since everything lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. Deleting a draft within the app removes it immediately. Deleting the app removes everything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to stephaniestreeting@gmail.com.
